# EducationCHV Organisation

### 🙋‍♀️ About Us
**EducationCHV** is a Community-Based Organization in Kakamega, Kenya, dedicated to empowering Community Health Volunteers (CHVs) through education, training, and digital tools.

We believe that a trained CHV can save a village. We bridge the gap between rural communities and quality healthcare by providing continuous learning, mentorship, and resources to frontline health heroes.

**Website:** https://bonymuseni.github.io/educationchv/

### 🌈 How to Contribute
We welcome everyone! Here's how you can get involved:

1.  **Health Professionals:** Volunteer to train CHVs or create simple health learning materials.
2.  **Developers & Designers:** Help us improve our website, learning app, and CHV reporting tools.
3.  **Donors & Partners:** Support a training, sponsor learning kits, or donate via M-Pesa.
4.  **Community Members:** Share our work, refer CHVs, and join our community outreaches.

To contribute to this repo: Fork > Edit > Pull Request. We review daily!
